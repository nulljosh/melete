# Melete Money

How Melete makes money. The company plan is `REVENUE.md` in the Code root; the ledger is `GTM.md`.

## Price and rail

Free on iPhone and Mac (ASC 6785162492, store name Thinkpond) and free on the web. The web has a $1 one-time unlock through Stripe Checkout (`api/posts.js`, `users.is_pro`). A $1 Stripe sale pays about $0.67. The native apps sell nothing and must not mention the paid tier, since App Review needs IAP for that.

## What is actually sold

The web unlock: one payment that turns on the account's Pro perks. The ideas in the feed are generated by Workers AI or written by the people who post. Nothing sold belongs to another publisher. The risk is quality, not copyright.

## Who pays and why

A person who posts and reads ideas on the web and wants the Pro perks once. One payment unlocks the account. There are no tiers and no tips.

## Cost to serve

Workers AI runs through `api/ai.js`. A cron Worker asks for one scheduled idea a day at 09:00. Enrich runs only when asked. Both are rate limited per IP. Email goes through the jaybulb-api mail path, with Resend as a fallback that bills per email. The repo sets no cap on Resend. Hosting is Cloudflare.
